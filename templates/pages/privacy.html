Privacy Policy

How we respect, protect, and handle your information at Kemi's Shop.

Information We Collect

When you browse our digital lookbook or contact us via WhatsApp, we only collect the information necessary to fulfill your orders and deliver your items:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, WhatsApp contact).
  • Delivery address and city for dispatch riders/couriers.
  • Order history, shortlisted wishlist preferences, and sizing requirements.

How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is used exclusively to communicate regarding your orders, arrange delivery, and provide personalized customer care. We never sell, rent, or share your contact information with unauthorized third parties.

Third-Party Services

When orders are dispatched, necessary delivery information (recipient name, address, and phone number) is provided only to verified delivery couriers or riders strictly for the purpose of package delivery.
